Ready today

The infrastructure is already in

This is the difference between buying land and buying a plot you can build on tomorrow. Every item below exists on site now.

Water and power to every plot

MSEB sanctioned power supply and a water connection run to each individual plot, not just to the gate.

Roads, compound and street lights

Wide internal roads, a compound around the layout, street lighting, and tar road access right up to the project.

1,000 plus trees, already grown in

Mango, cashew and bird-friendly shading species planted using the Miyawaki method, so your plot has canopy from day one.

Four trees on your own plot

Every plot comes with a mango, a cashew, a parijatak and a sonchafa tree already planted and maintained for you.

Two farm ponds

Rainwater harvesting into two ponds holding around seven lakh litres, which keeps the plantation green right through summer.

Complete documentation

Government NA order, sanctioned NA plan, individual 7/12 extract and registered sale deed, all handed over to you. See the full list.

The plots

74 plots, from 2,000 sq ft

Launched in February 2025, adjacent to our Resort Dapoli project which is now around 90 per cent sold. Plot sizes start at 2,000 sq ft and go to 3,000 sq ft and above, with wide internal roads and open spaces through the layout.

Bank loan is available for the plot and the bungalow together, so your holiday home and the land it sits on are financed as one.

Why Dapoli

A beach town and a hill station in one place

Dapoli sits on a plateau in Ratnagiri district with the coast below it: around thirty sightseeing spots, food worth driving for, and a town with a market, hospital and schools that makes a holiday home genuinely liveable.
