Reliable land investment in Dapoli

Buyers from Mumbai, Pune, the USA, the UK and Dubai own plots across our two projects. Here is what is drawing them to this stretch of Konkan.

What is driving Konkan land values

  1. Connectivity. The six lane Mumbai–Goa highway is already in. The proposed coastal highway would pass 5 to 6 km from our projects, with a RoRo ferry, a rail extension to Khed and the Panvel international airport all on the same corridor.
  2. Alibaug has priced out. The nearest comparable weekend market is saturated and expensive, which is pushing buyers further down the coast to Dapoli.
  3. Coastal land is finite. Geography and coastal regulation cap how much buildable land near this shoreline can ever exist.
  4. Remote work. A holiday home that absorbs four or five working days a month is worth far more to far more people than it was a decade ago.

Our track record on this land

Early investors in the adjacent Resort Dapoli project came in at around ₹250 per sq ft. Our current indicative rate is around ₹800 per sq ft, and leading developers are launching in this belt at ₹1,800 per sq ft and above. Resort Dapoli is now around 90 per cent sold.

That project also shows what the land turns into: 38 plots, 400 trees, a running maintenance team, a central kitchen and owners across seven countries. 1000 Trees is the same team, the same standard, on the plot next door.
