CHANGED FILES, every page now links to the icons:

  the 13 folder pages (project/, layout/, contact/ and so on)

All of them are in this zip in the right places. Upload the root files
and the folders, overwriting what is there.

THE TAB ICON appears as soon as you hard refresh.

THE GOOGLE ICON takes longer. Google only refreshes favicons when it
recrawls the site, usually a few days to a few weeks. You can speed it
up: in Google Search Console open URL Inspection, enter
https://www.1000treesdapoli.com/ and click Request Indexing.

Google requires the icon to be square, at least 48x48, and to stay at
the same address. Do not rename or move these files later.
