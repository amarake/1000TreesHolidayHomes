Dapoli guide

How to reach Dapoli

Close enough for a weekend from either city, and getting closer as the coastal highway and ferry projects come through.

From Mumbai by road

South on the Mumbai–Goa highway (NH66) through Panvel, Pen and Mangaon, then west towards Mandangad and Dapoli, around 230 km. The six lane upgrade on this corridor has already made a real difference to the drive.

From Pune by road

Via Tamhini Ghat, Mangaon and Mandangad, around 215 km. Tamhini is one of the best drives out of Pune, especially in the months after the monsoon.

By train

Khed on the Konkan Railway is the nearest station at about 40 km, roughly an hour by road. Chiplun is a little further with more services. Tell us your train and we will help arrange a pickup.

By bus

MSRTC state buses run from Mumbai and Pune including overnight services, and private operators run the same corridor.

What is coming

The proposed Mumbai–Goa coastal highway would pass just 5 to 6 km from our projects. A Mumbai–Dapoli RoRo ferry, the extension of the CST–Roha line to Mangaon and Khed, a four lane Pune–Mangaon road and the new international airport at Panvel are all on the map for this corridor. Each one brings Dapoli closer to both cities.

Common questions

How far is Dapoli from Mumbai?

Around 230 km by road, via the Mumbai–Goa highway through Panvel, Pen and Mangaon, then west towards Mandangad and Dapoli. An easy Friday evening or Saturday morning drive.

How far is Dapoli from Pune?

Around 215 km via Tamhini Ghat, Mangaon and Mandangad. One of the most scenic drives out of Pune, particularly in and just after the monsoon.

Which is the nearest railway station to Dapoli?

Khed on the Konkan Railway, about 40 km and roughly an hour by road. Chiplun is slightly further with more services.

Is there an airport near Dapoli?

Ratnagiri has limited service today. The new international airport at Navi Mumbai, Panvel, is around 165 km from Dapoli.

Will the drive get shorter?

Several projects point that way: the six lane Mumbai–Goa highway, a proposed Mumbai–Goa coastal highway that would pass 5 to 6 km from our projects, a proposed Mumbai–Dapoli RoRo ferry, extension of the CST–Roha line to Mangaon and Khed, and a proposed four lane road from Pune to Mangaon.
